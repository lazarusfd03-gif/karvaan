Stories & notes

Beyond the stage.

Thoughts on music, memory and the moments that bring people together.

The sound

Sufi, Punjabi & rock: one stage, many emotions.

The most memorable live sets move through more than one mood. Sufi melodies make space for reflection, Punjabi rhythms bring the room together, and rock gives familiar songs a new pulse. The magic is in the transition: knowing when to build, when to soften, and when to let a chorus belong to everyone in the room.

Celebrations

Choosing live music for an Indian wedding.

Music can follow the arc of a celebration — welcoming guests, lifting the energy, and creating a final memory that carries beyond the evening. A thoughtful live set considers the people in the room, the pace of the occasion and the songs that feel most like home.

The collective

Where rhythm becomes a conversation.

Every Karvaan performance is a conversation between melody and rhythm. Dholak and tabla create the foundation; guitar and keys colour the arrangement; vocals bring each song close to the audience. Together, the collective leaves room for each performance to be genuinely live.
